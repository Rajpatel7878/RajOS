# Success Criteria

RajOS will be considered successful when:

- It replaces multiple productivity apps.
- It is used every day.
- It helps achieve academic and career goals.
- It provides meaningful analytics.
- It remains fast, reliable, and maintainable.

---

# Long-Term Vision

RajOS should become a lifelong personal operating system that grows alongside its user.

It should evolve from a productivity dashboard into an intelligent platform for planning, tracking, analyzing, and improving every important aspect of life.
